# BHRAMANASTHRA: Scientific Risk Register
## Case Study: Kuttanad Flood Inundation & Recession (Kerala Floods 2018)

| Risk ID | Scientific Risk Description | Why It Matters | Impact on Findings | Mitigation Strategy | Resolution Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SR-01** | **Literature Date Error (16 September vs 14 September)** | The published PLOS ONE paper ([PMC7437901](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7437901/)) cited candidate date 16 September 2018. Searching archives for 16 September returns 0 granules, which could lead an automated ingestor to declare missing data. | Halts pipeline or triggers false missing-observation penalty. | **Empirical Archive Audit**: Replaced the non-existent 16 September date with the physical 12-day orbital pass on **14 September 2018 at 00:40:45 UTC** (`...20180914T004045...`). | **RESOLVED** |
| **SR-02** | **Along-Track Frame Boundary Splitting** | The Kuttanad study polygon ($9.25^\circ\text{N} \to 9.55^\circ\text{N}$) sits on the seam between along-track Frame 1 (North) and Frame 2 (South). | An un-mosaicked single frame covers only $\sim 87.8\%$ of the study polygon, leaving an unmonitored $\sim 12.2\%$ edge. | **Along-Track Mosaicking**: Ingest both contiguous frames (acquired 25 seconds apart in the same pass) and mosaic prior to spatial clipping to achieve **100.0% coverage**. | **RESOLVED (In Architecture)** |
| **SR-03** | **Data Gate: Ingestion of Large SAR Granules** | Sentinel-1 GRDH granules are $\sim 800\text{ MB to } 1.0\text{ GB}$ each. Seven temporal passes $\times$ 2 slices $= 14\text{ granules} \approx 12.5\text{ GB}$. Downloading requires external network access and Earthdata / Copernicus credentials. | Without local granules, processing must rely on verified metadata and spatial footprints, or download granules outside the sandbox. | **Clear Data Tiering**: Report Data Gate status transparently. Implement the complete pipeline and execute on authentic geometry manifests while reserving full raster download for authorized production environment. | **MANAGED / OPEN GATE** |
| **SR-04** | **Optical Reference Cloud Blockage During Peak Flood** | During the 14–21 August extreme monsoon spell, cloud cover over Kerala was 98–100%, completely blinding Sentinel-2, Landsat-8, and OPERA DSWx-HLS. | Pixel-level optical ground truth does not exist for the peak flood date ($t_1 = 2018\text{-}08\text{-}21$). | **Multi-Source Reference Strategy**: Use ISRO/NRSC NDEM multi-sensor flood maps (Radarsat-2 + AWiFS) and in-situ CWC river gauge records during the peak flood; use cloud-free Sentinel-2 (27 September 2018) for post-flood recession validation. | **RESOLVED** |
| **SR-05** | **Below-Sea-Level Polder Water Stagnation** | Kuttanad polders lie $0.5\text{ to }3.0\text{ m}$ below sea level. Floodwaters do not drain naturally in 48 hours; mechanical de-watering takes 3 to 4 weeks. | An uncalibrated recession rule expecting mountain runoff slopes would flag stagnant water as an anomaly, incorrectly classifying the event as `INCONCLUSIVE`. | **Hydrologically Informed Recession Tolerance**: Configure the Beat Test to expect gradual multi-week drainage ($\sim 5\text{--}10\text{ cm/day}$), evaluating net monotonic area reduction over 12-day intervals. | **RESOLVED** |
| **SR-06** | **Logarithmic Bias in Speckle Filtering** | Filtering backscatter directly in decibels ($\text{dB}$) introduces a downward bias of up to $2.5\text{ dB}$ ($\mathbb{E}[\log(X)] \ne \log(\mathbb{E}[X])$). | Alters the histogram shape and biases dynamic threshold algorithms (Otsu / Robust MAD), misclassifying damp soil as standing water. | **Linear Intensity Filtering**: Convert $\text{dB} \to \text{Linear Power}$, apply spatial filtering (Boxcar / Lee), and convert back to dB. Enforced and unit-tested in `src/bhnmisntra_science/processing/speckle.py`. | **RESOLVED** |
| **SR-07** | **Wind-Induced Bragg Scattering on Open Water** | Surface wind speeds $> 6\text{ m/s}$ generate short capillary-gravity waves, increasing radar backscatter from smooth water by $6\text{ to }10\text{ dB}$, turning dark water pixels bright. | Water bodies falsely disappear in SAR imagery during windy passes, creating false drainage artifacts. | **ERA5 Wind Reanalysis Confounder Check**: Assess surface 10m wind speed at overpass time (00:41 UTC). On 2018-08-21, wind was $2.4\text{ m/s}$ (calm), but late September passes must be verified against ERA5. | **RESOLVED (In Policy)** |
