## 1. Task 1: Scientific Validation of Assumptions

### 1.1 Sentinel-1 Radiometric Calibration Equations
- **Governing Physics & Documented Standard**:
  According to ESA authoritative specification *Radiometric Calibration of S-1 Level-1 Products Generated by the S-1 IPF* (Document No. S1-TN-MUC-GS-0001 / ESA-EOPG-CSCOP-TN-0002), the radar backscatter cross-section ($\sigma^0$) is calibrated from Level-1 Ground Range Detected (GRD) Digital Numbers ($DN$) via:
  $$\sigma^0_i = \frac{|DN_i|^2}{A_{\sigma, i}^2}$$
  $$\sigma^0_{\text{dB}, i} = 10 \log_{10}(\sigma^0_i) = 20 \log_{10}(|DN_i|) - 20 \log_{10}(A_{\sigma, i})$$
  where $A_{\sigma, i}$ is the range-dependent calibration factor extracted from the XML lookup table (`lutSigma0.xml`).
- **Validation against Implementation (`src/bhnmisntra_science/processing/calibration.py`)**:
  - `CalibratedLinearCalibration` converts linear power $\sigma^0_{\text{lin}} \to \text{dB}$ via $10 \log_{10}(\sigma^0_{\text{lin}})$: **`VERIFIED`**.
  - `CalibratedDbCalibration` passes through pre-calibrated dB without double-logarithm: **`VERIFIED`**.
  - `RawDnCalibration` applies $10 \log_{10}(DN^2)$ under the assumption of $A_{\sigma} = 1.0$. If raw uncalibrated `.SAFE` DNs are used directly, omitting the 2D range-varying vector $A_{\sigma}(x, y)$ produces a range-dependent radiometric tilt across the swath ($\sim 2\text{--}4\text{ dB}$).
- **Classification**:
  - Pre-calibrated pipeline paths (SNAP / RTC / GEE products in linear or dB): **`VERIFIED`**.
  - Raw uncalibrated `.SAFE` DN path without `lutSigma0.xml` vector ingestion: **`PROVISIONAL`** (Must require pre-calibrated GeoTIFFs or full XML lookup ingestion).

### 1.2 Slice Overlap vs. Slice Adjacency Audit
- **Phase 1.3 Hypothesis**:
  Phase 1.3 proposed applying an overlap feathering filter:
  $$I_{\text{mosaic}} = \frac{I_1 + I_2}{2} \quad \text{in the overlap zone.}$$
- **Empirical Vertex Audit**:
  Geodetic inspection of the authoritative ASF DAAC WKT footprints across all 7 dates reveals:
  - Southern boundary of North Slice on 2018-07-28: From $(75.605141^\circ\text{E}, 9.682505^\circ\text{N})$ to $(77.891197^\circ\text{E}, 9.225677^\circ\text{N})$.
  - Northern boundary of South Slice on 2018-07-28: From $(75.605217^\circ\text{E}, 9.682400^\circ\text{N})$ to $(77.891182^\circ\text{E}, 9.225589^\circ\text{N})$.
  - **Edge Discrepancy**: $\Delta \text{lon} \approx 0.000015^\circ\text{--}0.000076^\circ$ ($1.6\text{ to } 8.3\text{ meters}$), $\Delta \text{lat} \approx 0.000088^\circ\text{--}0.000105^\circ$ ($9.7\text{ to } 11.6\text{ meters}$).
- **Scientific Reality**:
  The two slices **DO NOT OVERLAP**. They are strictly **adjacent, abutting slices** cut along a single line split by the ESA Instrument Processing Facility (IPF). There is zero multi-pixel overlap buffer.
- **Rule Correction**:
  **Do NOT apply overlap blending.** Applying an overlap blending equation across abutting non-overlapping slices introduces unnecessary algorithmic branching and risks NaN seams. The slices must be assembled via **direct spatial concatenation / assignment onto the master UTM grid**:
  $$I_{\text{mosaic}}(x, y) = \begin{cases} 
  I_1(x, y) & \text{if } (x, y) \in \text{Footprint}_1 \\
  I_2(x, y) & \text{if } (x, y) \in \text{Footprint}_2 \\
  \text{NaN} & \text{otherwise}
  \end{cases}$$
- **Classification**:
  - Overlap Blending Hypothesis: **`FAILED`** (Disproven by footprint math).
  - Direct Abutting Slice Assembly Protocol: **`VERIFIED`**.

### 1.3 10m Pixel Spacing vs. Effective Spatial Resolution
- **Physical Distinction**:
  - **Pixel Spacing ($10.0\text{ m} \times 10.0\text{ m}$)**: The resampled digital raster grid spacing in range and azimuth.
  - **Effective Spatial Resolution ($20.4\text{ m} \times 22.5\text{ m}$)**: The physical half-power system point spread function (PSF) determined by radar chirp bandwidth ($44.3\text{ MHz}$) and 5-look range averaging ($\text{ENL} \approx 4.4$).
- **Impact on Minimum Mapping Unit (MMU)**:
  - An individual $10\text{ m}$ pixel is **not an independent physical measurement**. Neighboring pixels share ~50% spatial autocorrelation.
  - One independent spatial resolution cell is $\approx 20\text{ m} \times 22\text{ m} \approx 450\text{ m}^2$ ($\approx 4.5\text{ pixels}$).
  - A Minimum Mapping Unit (MMU) of 10 pixels corresponds to $1,000\text{ m}^2 \approx 2.2$ independent resolution cells. Setting MMU $< 5\text{ pixels}$ would detect single speckle fluctuations as false flood clusters.
- **Classification**: **`VERIFIED`** against ESA Sentinel-1 Product Specifications.

### 1.5 Analysis of Permanent-Water Masking Errors
Three concrete failure modes were identified when applying permanent-water masks in Kuttanad:

1. **Resolution & Grid Misalignment Staircases**:
   - The JRC Global Surface Water raster is provided at $30\text{ m}$ Landsat resolution. Resampling to a $10\text{ m}$ Sentinel-1 grid creates 1-to-2 pixel staircase artifacts along narrow meandering distributaries of the Pamba and Meenachil rivers.
   - *Mitigation*: Combine JRC with the native $10\text{ m}$ pre-flood Sentinel-1 baseline ($t_0 = \text{2018-07-28}$), identifying permanent water where $t_0 \le -18.0\text{ dB} \lor \text{JRC} > 80\%$.
2. **Rotational Aquaculture Polders ("Chemmeen Kettu")**:
   - In Lower Kuttanad, farmers rotate seasonal paddy with brackish-water shrimp and fish culture during the monsoon (May–October).
   - If a polder was actively filled for aquaculture during the 28 July baseline ($t_0$), masking it as "permanent water" blinds the algorithm to subsequent changes in that polder.
3. **Floating Macrophytes (Water Hyacinth Blooms)**:
   - Vembanad Lake and main drainage canals suffer from seasonal mats of floating African payal (*Eichhornia crassipes*).
   - Dense macrophyte mats cause diffuse volume scattering ($\sigma^0 \approx -10\text{ to } -12\text{ dB}$), appearing as dry land in baseline $t_0$. If flood currents flush the weeds away into the Arabian Sea, the exposed open water drops to $-20\text{ dB}$, masquerading as newly flooded dry land.
- **Classification**: Permanent water masking is **`PROVISIONAL`** and requires dual SAR baseline + optical validation guards.

## 2. Task 2: Temporal Logic & Four-State Model Validation

### 2.1 State Transition Definitions

The four-state temporal model tracks the area $A_i(t_k)$ of each candidate change cluster $i$ relative to previous observation $A_i(t_{k-1})$:

$$\Delta A_{\text{rel}} = \frac{A_i(t_k) - A_i(t_{k-1})}{A_i(t_{k-1})}$$

```
                                  ┌──────────────────────────────┐
                                  │      PEAK INUNDATION (t1)    │
                                  └──────────────┬───────────────┘
                                                 │
                     ┌───────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────┐
                     │                                                       │
                     ▼                                                       ▼
       ┌───────────────────────────┐                           ┌───────────────────────────┐
       │   PERSISTENT_STAGNATION   │                           │         RECEDING          │
       │    |ΔA_rel| <= 15%        │                           │     ΔA_rel < -15%         │
       │  Polder dykes trapped     │                           │  Pump / gravity drainage  │
       └─────────────┬─────────────┘                           └─────────────┬─────────────┘
                     │                                                       │
                     └───────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────┘
                                                 │
                     ┌───────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────┐
                     │                                                       │
                     ▼                                                       ▼
       ┌───────────────────────────┐                           ┌───────────────────────────┐
       │          DRAINED          │                           │        RE_INUNDATED       │
       │    A(tk) < MMU (10 px)    │                           │     ΔA_rel > +15%         │
       │   Exposed dry/moist bed   │                           └─────────────┬─────────────┘
       └───────────────────────────┘                                         │
                                                               ┌─────────────┴─────────────┐
                                                               ▼                           ▼
                                                          EXPLAINED                   UNEXPLAINED
                                                      (Rain >= 50 mm)                (Rain < 50 mm)
                                                        [COMPLIANT]                 [NON-COMPLIANT]
```

### 2.2 Sequence Compliance Criteria

1. **Persistent Stagnation Compliance**:
   - A cluster exhibiting `PERSISTENT_STAGNATION` during $s_1$ (+12d) and $s_2$ (+24d) evaluates as **`COMPLIANT`**. It correctly reflects below-sea-level elevation where water cannot drain until high-capacity axial pumps are energized.
2. **Gradual Recession Compliance**:
   - A cluster exhibiting monotonic or step-wise reduction ($> 15\%$ drop per interval) evaluates as **`COMPLIANT`**.
3. **Re-Inundation Compliance**:
   - If area increases by $> 15\%$, it evaluates as **`COMPLIANT WITH EXPLANATION`** if cumulative precipitation during the 12-day window is $\ge 50\text{ mm}$ (or high astronomical tide is logged).
   - If rainfall is $< 50\text{ mm}$, it evaluates as **`NON-COMPLIANT (UNEXPLAINED GROWTH)`**, reflecting an anomaly.
4. **Eventual Drainage Compliance**:
   - Once area drops below MMU ($10\text{ pixels} \approx 0.001\text{ km}^2$), the cluster reaches `DRAINED`.
5. **Hard PRD Gate Invariant ($N_{\text{valid}} \ge 3$)**:
   - If missing observations or geometry mismatch reduce the valid subsequent series to $N_{\text{valid}} < 3$, the system **strictly outputs `UNVERIFIED`**. Under no circumstance may an administrative override or partial model output `SUPPORTED`.
